Trends from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26

How have the environmental figures changed over the years?

NSE has a BRSR filing for Tata Steel for 4 of the 5 years from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26. FY 2021-22 has no filing of its own; its figures come from the previous-year column of the FY 2022-23 filing. The reporting basis changes during this period (consolidated in FY 2022-23 to standalone in FY 2023-24), so only years on the same basis are compared. 30 figures were restated in a later filing (marked ⟲).

How to read this page. Each column is one financial year, with the basis it was reported on (standalone = the company alone, consolidated = with its subsidiaries). “No filing” means NSE has none for that year: it is never shown as 0. ⟲ a later filing changed this year's figure. ⚠ the figure carries a note or looks doubtful. ≠ the next filing is on a different basis. u a different unit from the other years. Trend verdicts only compare years on the same basis and in the same unit, and only against the company's own earlier years.

Read this before comparing years

  • FY 2021-22: NSE has no BRSR filing for this year. The figures shown for FY 2021-22 are the previous-year column of the FY 2022-23 filing, so yes/no answers and texts are not available for it.
  • Reporting basis changed from “Consolidated basis” in FY 2022-23 to “Standalone basis” in FY 2023-24. Figures before and after the change describe different things, so trend verdicts only compare years on the same basis.
  • These years state no unit for some figures (older SEBI layout): FY 2021-22 (22 figures), FY 2022-23 (22 figures). They are shown as filed and not compared with years that state one.
  • 30 figures were changed by the filing of the following year (marked ⟲). Each figure is shown as filed in its own year; hover over ⟲ to see the later figure.
