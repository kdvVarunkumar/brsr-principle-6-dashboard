How is Reliance Industries doing on the environment?

In 2023-24 Reliance used 46.42 crore GJ of energy (2.0% less than last year) and released 3.77 crore tonnes of greenhouse gases (about the same). It took in 20.41 crore kL of water (1.8% more) and produced 6.66 lakh tonnes of waste (10.9% more), of which 97% was recycled or reused.

How to read “better” and “worse”: it only compares the company with itself last year. It is not a rating against other companies or legal limits; the filing does not contain those. A bigger business can use more in total, so we always show the figure per ₹ of sales next to the total.

Can I trust these numbers?

We never invent a number. Here is where each figure on this page came from, and the cases that need extra care.

Where the 80 numbers came from

  • 69 ✔ reported by the company, as filed
  • 3 ∑ added up by us from reported figures
  • 1 ⇄ unit changed by us (the original is kept)
  • 7 ∅ not reported (never shown as 0)
  • 10 ⚠ carry a note about the figure

Notes on this company’s figures

  • POP and HAP air pollutants are filed as 0, which may mean “not measured”.
  • Scope 3 emissions and the Scope 3 intensity are not reported.
  • Eight extra intensity figures (per tonne of product, or PPP-adjusted) have unclear unit labels. They stay in the SEBI tab, not here.

Why a figure may be flagged

  • The unit is missing or unusual.
  • A value looks far too big or too small for the company’s size.
  • Parts do not add up to the filed total.
  • A zero that may not be a real zero.

Flagged values are shown exactly as filed, never silently fixed.

Plain-English glossary

GJ (gigajoule)
A unit of energy. 1 GJ equals about 278 kWh, the “units” on an electricity bill.
kL (kilolitre)
1,000 litres of water, the same as fifty 20-litre water cans.
tCO₂e (tonnes of CO₂-equivalent)
One way to count all greenhouse gases together, by how much each warms the planet compared with CO₂.
Scope 1 / 2 / 3
Scope 1: released by the company itself. Scope 2: released by whoever makes the electricity it buys. Scope 3: everything else along its supply chain.
Intensity (“per ₹ of sales”)
The amount used or released for each ₹ of turnover. It lets you compare a company with itself even when it grows. We show it per ₹ 1 crore.
Lakh and crore
1 lakh = 1,00,000 (a hundred thousand). 1 crore = 1,00,00,000 (ten million).
Renewable energy
Energy from sources that do not run out, such as sun, wind and flowing water.
NOx, SOx, PM, VOC, POP, HAP
Air pollutants, listed on the Air quality card. None of them are greenhouse gases.
Zero liquid discharge
Treating and reusing all wastewater so none is released.
PAT scheme
“Perform, Achieve and Trade”: the Government of India’s energy-saving targets for big industrial sites.
EIA
Environmental Impact Assessment: a study of a project’s effect on nature, done before it is built.
Standalone vs consolidated
Standalone covers the company on its own. Consolidated adds its subsidiaries. The two should not be compared with each other.
