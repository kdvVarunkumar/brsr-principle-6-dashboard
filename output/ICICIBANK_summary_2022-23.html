What got better, and what got worse, in FY 2022-23?

We compared 5 figures for ICICI Bank between FY 2021-22 and FY 2022-23: 2 improved, 0 stayed about the same and 3 got worse. Biggest improvement: Waste produced was 66.1% less than last year. Biggest setback: Total energy used was 21.4% more than last year.

✔ 2 improved≈ 0 about the same✖ 3 got worse

How we decide what is “better”

  • “Better” only means better than the company's own figures for last year. The filings contain no industry benchmark or legal limit, so a company is never rated against others.
  • Each figure has a direction. Lower is better for energy, greenhouse gases, water and waste per ₹ of sales and for each air pollutant. Higher is better for the share of energy from renewable sources and the share of waste recycled or reused.
  • A change smaller than 1% (for a share: smaller than 0.5 of a percentage point) counts as “about the same” and is not ranked.
  • Amounts are ranked by how much they changed in percent. Shares are ranked by how many percentage points they changed (a rise from 80% to 90% counts as 10).
  • A company that grows will use more in total, so wherever a figure per ₹ of sales exists it is ranked instead of the total. The total is shown as context.
  • Both years come from the same filing, so they use the same reporting basis. A figure that is missing, doubtful, in a different unit or was zero last year is not ranked; the page lists it with the reason.
