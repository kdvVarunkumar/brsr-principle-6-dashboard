Laid out as in SEBI's Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting format (Annexure I, May 2021). Same question numbers, tables and row labels.

How to read the tables. Not reported = the filing has nothing for that item (it is never shown as 0). calc. = we added reported numbers together. conv. = we changed the unit. ⚠1 = a doubtful value, explained in the note under the table. Numbers are always shown exactly as filed unless marked. Units shown: energy in gigajoules (GJ), water in kilolitres (kL), waste and air pollutants in tonnes, greenhouse gases in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent (tCO₂e).
